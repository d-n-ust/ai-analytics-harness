Answerability · the left branch

Before any metric runs, a concept resolves to one of three states, decided by the graph, not the model. GOVERNED means a metric matches. COMPUTABLE means the parts are captured but no governed definition composes them, so any answer is authored. UNINSTRUMENTED means the warehouse does not hold it, which is decidable only because the spine asserts closure.
